## Diagnosis

The visual weight does not correspond to the importance of the information. The site repeats assertions about method while hiding the actual decisions inside long pages. A visitor should see a person with judgment, understand one relevant situation, inspect evidence, and start a context-rich conversation.

Source explains a credible cause of the overflow: .feature h3 uses viewport-relative sizing up to 4.8rem even inside a four-column card; metric labels share a row with large intrinsic-width values. These are source-supported hypotheses pending breakpoint reproduction. Hiding horizontal overflow is not an acceptable repair.

## Approaches considered

1. **Recommended — editorial engineering portfolio.** Quiet neutral surfaces, one restrained accent, confident but proportionate headings, substantial case summaries, concise first-person commentary. Best fit for professional trust and personal breadth.
2. **Technical fieldbook.** Denser navigation and dated notes with diagrams and decision records. Strong for peers; higher reading burden for nontechnical visitors.
3. **Portrait-led professional profile.** A real approved portrait, short introduction and conventional work cards. Accessible first impression, but easily generic if the work lacks distinctive detail.

Carry the editorial approach into visual exploration. Produce three actual visual alternatives using the captured source, then select one coherent direction before UI implementation. No invented photo, testimonial, diagram, client result, or logo relationship. The current orange/lime treatment and giant display type are not preservation requirements.

## Proposed experience

**Homepage sequence**
1. Suleman Manji; actual role at Viyu; a short scope line about M365 migrations, infrastructure and service automation. Keep an approachable first-person voice.
2. Primary action “Explore my work” → /projects; secondary “Start a conversation” → /work-with-me. Resume remains directly available in navigation.
3. One or two selected cases, each organized as situation → my responsibility → consequential decision → supported outcome → read the case. Until new cases are approved, use existing public evidence without fabricating replacements.
4. One short “How I work” example linked to a real decision in a case. The label alone is not proof of method.
5. A brief career/personal passage and two selected writing or experiment links. Leave room for curiosity without repeating the entire project catalog or timeline.
6. Contextual conversation link with visible Viyu role. No forced service catalog.

**Projects:** featured delivery narratives first, then inspectable public tools, then experiments. Use descriptive titles; put maturity, date, and repository/version details in a separate metadata line. Prefer one or two roomy columns for substantive summaries.

**Case pages:** compact opening with situation, personal role, delivery state and result/limit; readable narrative with section links; one real decision illustrated only when the evidence supports it; scenario-specific next conversation.

**Contact:** implement #16's person-led scenario routing. No generic “business-grade” dismissal. A visitor should not have to know the employer's sales process before reaching the engineer.

**Personal recognition:** preserve About, Experience, Resume, Writing and Beyond Work routes. Do not rewrite personal history as sales copy or fill empty space with generic engineering slogans.

## Visual rules for selection and implementation

- One typography hierarchy throughout. Starting bounds: body 17–18px desktop / 16–18px mobile, line-height 1.5–1.7; hero 40–64px desktop / 32–40px mobile; case/card headings 22–30px. Adjust within the selected visual, not independently per page.
- Prose approximately 60–75 characters wide; metadata is secondary but readable. Use normal case for explanatory labels.
- Neutral background and ink; one accent for links, active states and limited emphasis. Remove the animated domain strip and the full-height metrics billboard.
- Repeated spacing scale and aligned content edges. Cards get room appropriate to their text; short summary links may use compact rows.
- No mandatory viewport-height hero, fixed card height, manual word-by-word line breaks or arbitrary vertical spacer.
- Counts appear only where they explain scope, with their original date and qualification. Never let a number outrank the case it supports.
- Real approved personal imagery is optional. Personality can come from edited first-person writing and specific choices; stock server racks and generated “engineer at laptop” imagery do not establish identity.
- Preserve theme preference consistently on home and inner pages, with verified foreground/background pairs and stable readable loading states.
