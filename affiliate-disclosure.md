# Affiliate disclosure

Some Publications contain affiliate or referral links. I may earn a commission if you buy or sign up through them. These relationships do not change the editorial standard or the price you pay.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Sponsored work names the sponsor and states the material support. A paid recommendation names the payer. A Publication about a product or service that I created states my ownership before the offer.
