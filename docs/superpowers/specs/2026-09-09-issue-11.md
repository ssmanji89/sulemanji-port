The recommendation is an inference from audience fit, not a measured conversion finding. Gavin Stone and Ben Whitmore are the closest employed-professional structural comparisons. Nick Ross, Jim Norman and Laura Thrasher are useful for problem/specialty clarity, but their independent commercial models should not be copied.

## Proposed design
1. **Introduction:** name, actual role, recognizable delivery specialties; explain newer agent tooling as part of current practice rather than the mechanism behind the entire career.
2. **Professional evidence:** start with the best supported M365 acquisition/migration case; add RFMS when it contributes a distinct situation and engineering decision. Each begins with context and the actual personal role.
3. **Evidence types:** distinguish delivered work, reusable community artifacts, and experiments. Do not use activity counts as the primary substitute for delivery evidence.
4. **Conversation path:** case or homepage → scenario-based Contact → organizational engagement through Viyu, professional opportunity/collaboration with Suleman, or personal project. Keep Suleman central and make the actual provider explicit. Static contact links suffice initially.
5. **Personal breadth:** preserve résumé/career access, projects, writing and Beyond Work. Do not turn the whole homepage into a service catalog.
6. **Inspectable method:** each case includes one source-backed decision/validation example, sanitized and labeled accurately. No raw logs or new recurring publishing obligation.
7. **Public text:** approved claims only; make dates, role, scope and source visible where useful. Machine-readable text must remain accurate without counters or JavaScript.
