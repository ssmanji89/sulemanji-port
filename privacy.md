# Privacy Notice

Version: 2026-07-11

This notice covers the public portfolio site and Work With Me intake.

## Work With Me data

AI participates in discovery and blueprint generation, and Suleman may review any thread.

Customers must not submit secrets, credentials, attachments, regulated records, sensitive third-party data, or material they lack authority to share.

Customer text is sent only to processors required for the service and is not used for unrelated model training by this application.

Logs exclude raw email bodies, secrets, OAuth tokens, full prompts, and chain-of-thought.

D1 stores structured summaries and message identifiers rather than complete mailbox copies where practical.

## Retention

Working intake content, discovery state, and generated working material are deleted or irreversibly redacted 90 days after case closure.

The labeled Gmail thread is archived at closure and deleted one year later.

Stripe remains authoritative for financial records. D1 retains only the identifiers, consent evidence, credit history, and transaction metadata required for operations, disputes, tax, and legal obligations.

A customer may request earlier deletion subject to payment-dispute, fraud-prevention, tax, and legal recordkeeping requirements.

Retention jobs produce auditable success/failure events and alert on missed deletion deadlines.

## Contact

For privacy requests, use the email link in the site footer.
